Our Approach

Our framework empowers physically simulated characters to execute multi-agent human-object interaction (HOI) tasks with naturalness and precision.

Our framework employs a two-phase learning paradigm. In the first phase, depicted on the left, we train single-agent carrying skills using the AMP framework. In the second phase, we transfer these single-agent skills to a cooperative context. Notably, we use the dynamics of the object as feedback information and implicit communication channel, as illustrated by the bounding box in the figures.
